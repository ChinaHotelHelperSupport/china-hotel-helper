Privacy Policy — China Hotel Helper

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Summary: China Hotel Helper uses local planning data first. If no local scenario matches, an optional online recommendation can send your question and selected travel options to our Cloudflare backend and DeepSeek after you agree in the extension. No account or login is required. The separate “Plan my whole trip” feature is processed locally.

1. Who we are

China Hotel Helper is an independent Chrome extension providing accommodation-area planning guidance for international travelers visiting China. It is not operated by or endorsed by Trip.com. Its affiliate relationship with Trip.com is described below.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Your most recent hotel-planning request, selected destination and travel type, and local result-display state. Your most recent recommendation, including an online-generated answer when used, is saved locally on your device so it can be displayed again when you reopen the extension.“Clear saved search” removes the saved request and recommendation.
  • Your saved trip-plan form, including cities, nights, places, arrival and departure details, and selected airports or stations.
  • A randomly generated installation identifier used to group online requests for usage limits. This is not a verified personal identity or a login account.

These records use Chrome extension local storage. Saving a request locally does not mean it is never transmitted: the online recommendation flow described below can send the same question after you agree.

3. Optional online recommendations

When a local scenario does not cover a hotel request, the extension presents a notice and an unchecked agreement box. If you agree and submit the request again, the extension sends your question, selected destination, selected travel type, and installation identifier to our backend hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

The backend sends the question and selected travel options to the DeepSeek API to generate accommodation-area guidance. The application does not include the installation identifier or your connection IP address in the question sent to DeepSeek. DeepSeek receives the connection from our backend rather than a direct API call from your browser.

The extension does not require your name, email address, passport number, payment details, Trip.com credentials, or booking reference. Do not put sensitive information into the free-text field: anything you type there may be included in an online request.

Local scenario matches do not trigger this DeepSeek recommendation request. The separate “Plan my whole trip” form is not sent through this online recommendation flow.

4. Connection information and usage limits

Cloudflare processes network requests to the backend, including the connection IP address and technical request information. The application uses the connection IP address and installation identifier to enforce usage limits.

Before storing quota keys, the application derives day-specific HMAC values using a server-held secret. The quota record contains these derived values and request counts, not the original question, raw IP address, or raw installation identifier. These values are pseudonymous and should not be understood as guaranteed anonymous data.

Online recommendations are subject to per-installation,network and service-wide usage limits. Daily allowances reset at midnight Hong Kong time. Limits may be adjusted to manage capacity and prevent abuse. Short-term installation and network limits also apply. A reserved request counts even if generation later fails. Local planning guidance remains available when online requests are unavailable.

5. Storage, retention, and service providers

The recommendation handler processes questions to obtain an answer; its quota database is not designed to store question or answer histories. The latest quota-day record is replaced when a new day's request is successfully reserved. It is not automatically deleted at midnight if no subsequent request is accepted.

Cloudflare may separately retain operational logs and infrastructure records according to the service configuration and its policies. DeepSeek may process and retain API inputs and outputs under its applicable API terms and privacy practices. Clearing local extension data does not erase records already processed by those providers. Provider processing may occur outside your country or region.

Provider information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy; DeepSeek website and applicable privacy/API terms.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can decline online processing by leaving the agreement box unchecked and using local guidance instead. The agreement is currently held only in the open popup; it is not saved as permanent consent. Closing and reopening the popup requires agreement again before a new online request.

“Clear saved search” removes the saved search, and “Clear saved trip” removes the saved trip form. These controls do not remove the installation identifier or previously processed server records. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Contact support about server-side data questions; without a login or the relevant identifying details, it may not be possible to associate a request with a particular person.

7. Trip.com affiliate links

When you choose the Trip.com button, the extension opens an affiliate link in a new tab. If you complete an eligible booking, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

The extension does not automatically open Trip.com, inject affiliate links into other webpages, or modify Trip.com webpages. The current fixed affiliate link does not include your free-text question, saved trip plan, or installation identifier.

Trip.com and its affiliate services may process referral information, cookies, and activity under their own policies after you follow the link. Review those policies and booking terms before making a reservation.

8. Purposes and sharing

Application data is used to provide planning guidance, save your local preferences, enforce usage limits, and operate the service. Online inputs are shared with Cloudflare and DeepSeek as described above. We do not sell hotel-planning questions to advertisers or use them to build advertising profiles.

9. Security and recommendation limitations

Online requests use HTTPS. The DeepSeek API key and quota-hashing secret are stored on the backend, not supplied to users in the extension. No electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Recommendations are planning guidance, not verified live hotel search results. Confirm hotel location, current transport arrangements, prices, availability, and booking conditions before payment.

10. Children

This tool is intended for travelers able to consent to the relevant data processing under applicable law. It is not directed to children. Contact support if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the extension or its data handling changes. Material changes requiring further notice or consent will be addressed in the product before the affected processing begins.
